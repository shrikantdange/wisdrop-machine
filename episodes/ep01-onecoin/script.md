Conventions
- VO writes every number as words; the figure appears on screen in the same form with its source line. Dates spoken in narration appear in the card dateline or source line (small mono), and are not counted against the 3-figures-per-minute cap; big figure cards are.
- Real people are named only in narration and on text cards. The founder is never drawn; stage shots show an EMPTY spotlight. Rosa, Victor, Dee and Theo are fictional composites (cast.md).
- Claims about the founder are attributed ("prosecutors say", "the F.B.I. believes"); she has not been tried.
- The FCA notice is called a warning, not a fine. Sentences are final sentences; nothing here is "proposed".
- SOURCE lines cite research.md rows (F01...) where the URLs live. "SAY" gives the voice a phonetic version; captions use the VO text.
- Every story shot carries the DRAMATISATION tag. 1 s near-silence before the twist (line 073, ~5:23) and before each act question.

## Three hook versions (cold open, first ~30 s)

**Hook A — The vanishing (PICK).** Departure board, SOFIA → ATHENS, 25 Oct 2017; "Then she vanished." → "$4B+" by ~0:08 → "3.5M" victims and Rosa by ~0:15 → FBI Ten Most Wanted → "So how do you sell millions of people a coin that never existed?" Why: the strongest real moment, a mystery with a body count of savings, and it pays off in the Fall when the same board returns. Sourced to FBI, State Dept, DOJ SDNY, Reuters.

**Hook B — The arena.** "In June twenty sixteen, thousands of people cheered a new coin at Wembley Arena. / The founder called it the Bitcoin killer. / There was just one problem. / The coin didn't exist." → "$4B+", "3.5M" → "So how do you sell a coin that isn't there?" Strong visual (empty spotlight, sparks) but the founder's stage moment is harder to show without drawing her.

**Hook C — The dashboard.** "Imagine checking your savings every morning and watching them only go up. / For two years, that's what millions of OneCoin investors saw. / Every one of those numbers was set by the company. / Then the founder got on a plane." → "$4B+", "3.5M" → question. Most relatable ("you" in line one) but slower to the shocking moment.

Pick: **A**. The script below uses Hook A.
