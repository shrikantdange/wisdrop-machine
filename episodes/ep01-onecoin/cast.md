# Cast and sets: OneCoin — The Coin That Never Existed (ep01)

Status: draft for owner approval (playbook section 5, 5.1, 5.2, 6.1).

## Rules for this episode
- All four characters are FICTIONAL composites. None is modelled on, named after, or styled like any real person in research.md. No real logos, no real faces.
- The real founder is NEVER drawn, not even as a silhouette or a stand-in. Wherever the story needs her, the frame shows documents (FBI list card, reward card, email cards) or an EMPTY spotlight on a stage. VICTOR is a regional seminar host in Rosa's network, not a portrait of any OneCoin leader.
- Other real people (co-founder, brother, compliance head, lawyer, real victims) appear only as names on evidence cards and in narration.
- Characters never speak or lip-sync. They act through pose, expression and props.
- House style: adult proportions about 7 heads tall, realistic hands, calm believable faces, flat-vector with 2-3 tones per material, one hard shadow shape, paper grain. Clothing in muted navy, charcoal, beige, olive, rust or burgundy, plus one signature accent each.
